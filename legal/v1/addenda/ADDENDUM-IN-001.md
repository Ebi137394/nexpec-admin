# India — Country Addendum

> **Plain-English summary.** This is the India overlay on the NEXPEC legal stack. India's DPDP Act 2023 is in roll-out; GST registration via Stripe Tax is required for Indian-paid Inspectors; the IT Act 2000 intermediary safe-harbour requires platform-side procedures (grievance officer, takedown timelines).

## 1. Trigger
This Addendum applies whenever any of the following is true: the user is resident in India; the user is a business registered in India; or the Job is performed in India.

## 2. Consumer Protection — Consumer Protection Act 2019
For Job Contracts where the Client is an individual consumer (not a business) resident in India:
- **E-commerce rules.** NEXPEC complies with the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**, including the grievance-officer requirement, the redressal timeline (acknowledge within 48 hours, resolve within one month), and the mandatory disclosures to consumers about platform terms and dispute mechanisms.
- **Grievance officer.** NEXPEC's India Grievance Officer is reachable at **grievance@nexpec.com**. Replacement: **[TBD before IN market activation; appoint a designated officer per the rules]**.

## 3. Data Protection — DPDP Act 2023
For personal data of India-resident **Data Principals**:
- **DPDP compliance.** NEXPEC complies with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** ("**DPDP Act**") and its implementing rules and notifications as they come into effect.
- **Notice & consent.** Notice is provided to Indian Data Principals in clear and plain language identifying the personal data processed, the purposes, and the rights available; consent is obtained via the platform consent flow.
- **Cross-border transfer.** Cross-border transfer of personal data is permitted under the DPDP Act except to countries notified by the Central Government as restricted; NEXPEC maintains an up-to-date assessment of restricted destinations in the subprocessor list.
- **Child data.** Processing of personal data of individuals under 18 requires verifiable parental consent; the platform is not directed at minors.
- **Data principal rights.** Indian Data Principals may exercise rights of access, correction, completion, updating, and erasure, and may nominate a person to exercise rights on their behalf, via privacy@nexpec.com.

## 4. IT Act 2000 — Intermediary Safe-Harbour
NEXPEC qualifies as an **intermediary** under Section 2(1)(w) of the **Information Technology Act, 2000** and complies with the conditions for safe-harbour under Section 79 and the **IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021**:
- Publishing of terms of use and privacy policy in conspicuous locations;
- Designation of a Grievance Officer for India (see §2);
- Compliance with takedown directions under Section 79(3) within the prescribed timelines;
- Annual reporting and prohibited-content rules where applicable.

## 5. Language
English. Hindi translations of consumer-facing legal documents are provided on request; no statutory mandate.

## 6. Tax — GST + OIDAR
Stripe administers Indian **Goods and Services Tax** (CGST + SGST or IGST for cross-border) registration, invoicing, and remittance via Stripe Tax. For B2C cross-border digital services, **OIDAR (Online Information & Database Access or Retrieval)** registration may be required for NEXPEC where IN-resident consumers are served and Stripe-side thresholds are met; Stripe Tax administers OIDAR where applicable. Inspectors operating in India are independently responsible for their own GST registration and reporting per INSP-AGR-001 §2.

## 7. Payment Services — RBI Considerations
Stripe handles INR payments and complies with **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** guidelines on payment aggregators and recurring auto-debit mandates. NEXPEC is not a payment aggregator under RBI rules.

## 8. Dispute Resolution — Consumer-Jurisdiction Carve-out
TOS-001 §10 governs disputes (mandatory 30-day mediation → exclusive Montréal courts), **except** that an India-resident consumer may file a complaint with the **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission** of the consumer's domicile under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 for non-waivable consumer rights, and may bring claims in the **competent court at the consumer's place of residence** per Section 17 of the Consumer Protection Act. The mandatory 30-day mediation step still applies as a procedural prerequisite where local mandatory law permits.

## 9. Industry-Specific
For Jobs performed in India, Inspectors are responsible for their own certifications under **BIS**, **PESO** (petroleum and explosives), **DGMS** (mining), **AERB** (atomic energy), **Factories Act 1948**, and sector-specific regulators. NEXPEC does **not** verify India-sector-specific entitlement beyond the baseline document-validity check in TOS-001 §4.
