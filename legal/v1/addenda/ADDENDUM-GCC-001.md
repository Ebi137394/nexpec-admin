# GCC — Country Addendum (KSA, UAE, Qatar)

> **Plain-English summary.** This is the GCC overlay covering Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar. The three jurisdictions share enough commonalities — Arabic-language preferences, similar data-protection architectures, sector-specific industrial licensing — to be grouped, but each has its own mandatory-overlay slots. Two business actions are required before activating any GCC market: Arabic translation of the consumer-facing legal pack, and KSA NDMO controller registration.

## 1. Trigger
This Addendum applies whenever any of the following is true: the user is resident in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, or Qatar; the user is a business registered in any of those three jurisdictions; or the Job is performed in any of those three jurisdictions. Sub-sections call out jurisdiction-specific obligations where they differ.

## 2. Data Protection — Three Regimes
- **Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.** NEXPEC complies with the **Personal Data Protection Law** ("PDPL", Royal Decree M/19) and its implementing regulations issued by the **National Data Management Office (NDMO)**. Cross-border transfer of KSA-resident personal data is permitted under PDPL Article 29 with appropriate safeguards; NEXPEC relies on contractual safeguards equivalent to the EU SCCs.
- **United Arab Emirates.** NEXPEC complies with **Federal Decree-Law No. (45) of 2021** on the Protection of Personal Data ("UAE PDPL"). UAE residents retain rights of access, correction, transfer, and erasure. Where the user's data is collected by a UAE-registered entity, NEXPEC acts as a processor under UAE PDPL Articles 5–10.
- **Qatar.** NEXPEC complies with **Law No. 13 of 2016** concerning Personal Data Privacy Protection (PDPPL). Qatar-resident users may exercise rights under PDPPL Articles 4–7.
- **Subprocessor list.** The current subprocessor list and transfer-mechanism status for GCC residents is published at nexpec.com/legal/subprocessors.

## 3. Language — Arabic
Arabic is the official language of legal proceedings in KSA, the UAE, and Qatar. **For KSA-resident consumers, an Arabic-language version of the consumer-facing legal pack (TOS-001, PRIV-001, AUP-001, and the applicable Tier-2 Role Agreement) is provided.** For UAE and Qatar, Arabic versions are provided on request; in case of conflict between an executed Arabic version and the English original, the executed Arabic version controls.

## 4. Sharia Overlay — Late-Payment Interest & Penalties
NEXPEC does **not** charge late-payment interest, **riba**, or compound penalties to any user. The **10% PFT Fee** is a fixed platform-facilitation-and-technology fee paid for software access, matching, and escrow services — it is not consideration for the use of money, and is not a financing or credit product. Refunds and reversals operate on a return-of-principal basis under ESCROW-001 §5. This Addendum is consistent with the operating principles of Islamic finance in the GCC markets.

## 5. Employment & Visa
For Inspectors performing Jobs in KSA, the UAE, or Qatar:
- **Independent contractor status reaffirmed.** NEXPEC is not the employer of any Inspector, does not sponsor any Inspector's visa, and does not act as a labour-supply or recruitment intermediary.
- **Kafala / sponsorship.** Inspectors operating in the GCC under sponsored-employment regimes (Kafala) are responsible for their own visa and labour-permit compliance with their sponsor. NEXPEC declines all responsibility for the Inspector's immigration, residency, or visa status.
- **Saudi Wage Protection / WPS-equivalent regimes.** These regimes apply between an Inspector and the Inspector's sponsor; they do not implicate NEXPEC as a non-party.

## 6. Tax — VAT
- **KSA.** ZATCA-administered VAT (15%). Stripe Tax administers KSA VAT registration where applicable.
- **UAE.** Federal Tax Authority VAT (5%). Stripe Tax administers UAE VAT registration where applicable.
- **Qatar.** No VAT currently in effect; corporate income-tax obligations of Qatar-registered entities are independent of NEXPEC.

Inspectors operating in GCC are independently responsible for their own VAT registration and reporting where local thresholds apply.

## 7. Dispute Resolution — GCC Carve-out (Optional)
TOS-001 §10 governs disputes (mandatory 30-day mediation → exclusive Montréal courts). **For business users only**, the parties may by mutual written agreement substitute arbitration before:
- **DIFC-LCIA Arbitration Centre** (Dubai International Financial Centre, English-language proceedings); or
- **ADGM Arbitration Centre** (Abu Dhabi Global Market); or
- **Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration (SCCA)** (Riyadh, Arabic and English).

The mandatory 30-day mediation step still applies as a procedural prerequisite. No GCC arbitration substitution is available against an individual consumer.

## 8. Industry-Specific
For Jobs performed in the GCC, Inspectors are responsible for their own certifications under the relevant sector regulator: **ADNOC**, **ADNOC Onshore/Offshore**, **PDO**, **SABIC**, **Saudi Aramco**, **Saudi HSCE**, **Qatar Energy**, **Qatar Petroleum**. NEXPEC does **not** verify GCC-sector-specific entitlement beyond the baseline document-validity check in TOS-001 §4.

## 9. Anti-Corruption Representation
Each user warrants compliance with applicable anti-corruption laws (including but not limited to the **UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021** on the issuance of the Crimes and Penalties Law, the **KSA Anti-Bribery Law**, the **US FCPA**, and the **UK Bribery Act**) in connection with any Job performed in or paid from the GCC. NEXPEC, its personnel, and its agents do not solicit, accept, or offer any improper payment in connection with the Platform.
