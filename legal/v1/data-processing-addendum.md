# NEXPEC — Data Processing Addendum

> **Plain-English summary.** This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is the controller-processor contract between your Organization (the Controller) and NEXPEC (the Processor). It's required by GDPR Article 28, UK GDPR, Québec Law 25, and other major privacy laws. The Organization Agreement (ORG-AGR-001 §4) hooks this DPA into the master stack. The DPA spells out what data NEXPEC processes for you, what we're allowed to do with it, how we secure it, where it goes, who else touches it, and what happens at the end.

## 1. Parties, Subject Matter & Duration
This DPA is entered into between:
- **Customer ("Controller"):** the legal entity that accepted ORG-AGR-001 on the Platform; and
- **NEXPEC Technologies ("Processor")**, of Montréal, Québec, Canada.

The DPA forms part of the Organization Agreement and applies to processing of personal data of identified or identifiable individuals ("**Personal Data**") that the Controller, or any of its Seats, transmits to or has processed through the Platform. The DPA is in force for so long as ORG-AGR-001 is in force, plus the post-termination retention period defined in §10.

## 2. Nature, Purpose & Scope of Processing
NEXPEC processes Personal Data **only** to:
1. provide the Platform services described in TOS-001 and ORG-AGR-001;
2. facilitate Jobs (matching, contract generation, escrow, communications, audit);
3. comply with applicable laws and reasonable instructions from the Controller; and
4. exercise its own legitimate interests in Platform security, fraud prevention, and improvement (limited to anonymized/aggregated use).

The categories of Personal Data, categories of Data Subjects, and processing operations are set out in **Annex A** to this DPA.

## 3. Controller / Processor Roles
- The **Controller** determines the purposes and means of processing of Personal Data uploaded by the Organization's Seats. The Controller warrants that it has a lawful basis under applicable law to disclose Personal Data to NEXPEC.
- **NEXPEC is the Processor** with respect to that data. NEXPEC processes only on the Controller's documented instructions (this DPA, ORG-AGR-001, and any subsequent written instructions delivered via support@nexpec.com or the Platform's data-protection settings).
- Where NEXPEC is independently the **Controller** of certain data (e.g., account-credential data, fraud-prevention signals, security telemetry), that processing is governed by PRIV-001.

## 4. Processor Obligations
NEXPEC will:
1. Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to international transfers, unless legally compelled otherwise (and will notify the Controller of any such compulsion where law permits);
2. Ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations;
3. Implement appropriate **technical and organizational measures** consistent with **Annex B** (Security Measures);
4. Assist the Controller, taking into account the nature of the processing, by appropriate technical and organizational measures, in fulfilling responses to **Data Subject rights requests**;
5. Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with security, breach notification, **data-protection impact assessment**, and prior-consultation obligations;
6. At the choice of the Controller, **delete or return** all Personal Data after the end of the provision of services, subject to retention required by applicable law (see §10);
7. Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to **audits** as described in ORG-AGR-001 §5.

## 5. Subprocessors
- The Controller **generally authorizes** NEXPEC to engage Subprocessors, provided that NEXPEC imposes data-protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than those imposed on NEXPEC by this DPA.
- The current list of Subprocessors is published at **nexpec.com/legal/subprocessors** and notified in-app on material changes.
- The Controller may, within **fifteen (15) business days** of notification of a new Subprocessor, object on reasonable data-protection grounds; NEXPEC will work in good faith to resolve the objection or, if not resolved, the Controller may terminate the affected Platform service with a pro-rated refund.

## 6. International Transfers
For Personal Data transferred from the **EU/EEA** to NEXPEC (Canada) and/or Subprocessors, the **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), **Module Two — Controller to Processor**, are incorporated by reference into this DPA, with the docking clauses completed by reference to **Annex C**. For transfers from the **United Kingdom**, the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA, February 2022)** or the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs (as applicable) is incorporated. For transfers from **Canada / Québec**, NEXPEC relies on contractual safeguards consistent with **Law 25 §17** and **PIPEDA**. A **Transfer Impact Assessment** is published at nexpec.com/legal/transfer-impact-assessment.

## 7. Data Subject Rights
NEXPEC will provide reasonable technical and organizational assistance to enable the Controller to respond to Data Subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent). Where a Data Subject submits a request directly to NEXPEC, NEXPEC will, where lawful, redirect the Data Subject to the Controller and notify the Controller without undue delay.

## 8. Personal Data Breach
NEXPEC will notify the Controller **without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours** of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data processed for the Controller. The notification will include the information required by applicable law (nature of breach, categories and approximate numbers affected, likely consequences, measures taken).

## 9. Audits
The Controller may audit NEXPEC's compliance with this DPA on the terms set out in **ORG-AGR-001 §5** — namely, with reasonable advance notice and not more than once per twelve months absent a security incident or regulatory inquiry. Audits will be conducted in a manner that does not unduly disrupt NEXPEC's operations or compromise the confidentiality of other customers' data.

## 10. Term, Deletion & Return
On termination of ORG-AGR-001, NEXPEC will, at the Controller's documented election, **delete** or **return** all Personal Data processed on behalf of the Controller, subject to retention required by applicable law (including tax, audit, and litigation-hold obligations described in PRIV-001 §8). The Controller's election must be received within **sixty (60) days** of termination; absent timely election, NEXPEC will delete on its standard retention schedule. The 60-day election window is intended to accommodate enterprise-customer compliance cycles (vendor offboarding reviews, security exit audits, regulatory archival certifications) without indefinite storage exposure for NEXPEC.

## 11. Liability
The liability cap and exclusions in TOS-001 §9 and ORG-AGR-001 §9 apply to claims under this DPA, subject to mandatory rules of applicable data-protection law that override contractual liability limitations.

## 12. Order of Precedence
In case of conflict, the order of precedence is: (1) a signed Order Form expressly modifying this DPA; (2) this DPA; (3) the Privacy Policy (PRIV-001); (4) ORG-AGR-001; (5) TOS-001.

## 13. Governing Law
This DPA is governed by the laws of the Province of Québec, Canada, except where mandatory data-protection law of the Controller's jurisdiction requires otherwise.

## Annex A — Categories of Personal Data & Data Subjects
**Categories of Data Subjects.** Controller's personnel and Seats; Inspector and Agency users matched with Controller's Jobs; on-site personnel referenced in Inspector Output.
**Categories of Personal Data.** Identification and contact details; professional credentials; Job-related communications; geolocation associated with Job sites; inspection reports and findings; ratings; device, log, and security telemetry.
**Purposes.** Job matching, contract generation, escrow facilitation, communications, dispute support, audit, security, statutory recordkeeping.

## Annex B — Technical & Organizational Measures (TOMs)
TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, Supabase Row-Level Security, role-based access control, audited admin actions, secret rotation, SSO for personnel access, principle of least privilege, formal incident-response procedure, vendor risk reviews. The current TOMs schedule is published at nexpec.com/legal/security.

## Annex C — SCCs Docking
**SCCs Module Two — Controller to Processor (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914).** Data exporter: the Controller. Data importer: NEXPEC. Competent supervisory authority: the supervisory authority of the Controller's Member State, or the EDPS where applicable. Annexes I.A, I.B, II, III completed by reference to Annexes A and B of this DPA and the Subprocessor list at nexpec.com/legal/subprocessors.
