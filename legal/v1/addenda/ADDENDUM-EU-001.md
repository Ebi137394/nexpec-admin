# European Union / EEA — Country Addendum

> **Plain-English summary.** This is the EU/EEA overlay on the NEXPEC legal stack. NEXPEC operates from Canada, so cross-border-transfer mechanics, GDPR Article 28 controller-processor rules, EU consumer-jurisdiction rules, and the Platform-to-Business Regulation all need explicit overlay. NEXPEC must also appoint an Article 27 EU Representative before activating the EU market — this is a business action separate from this drafting.

## 1. Trigger
This Addendum applies whenever any of the following is true: the user is resident in an EU/EEA Member State; the user is a business established in an EU/EEA Member State; or the Job is performed in an EU/EEA Member State.

## 2. Consumer Protection — Right of Withdrawal
For Job Contracts where the Client is an individual consumer (not a business) resident in the EU/EEA, the **Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU)** applies. Specifically:
- The consumer has a **fourteen (14) day right of withdrawal** from the Job Contract from the date of acceptance, without giving any reason.
- The right of withdrawal is **expressly waived** by the consumer at confirmation of hire **only where the Inspector commences performance before expiry of the withdrawal period and the consumer has been informed of this consequence** (CRD Article 16(a)).
- Where the right is exercised before commencement, the Client is refunded the full Contract Value (less Stripe processing fees Stripe will not return).

## 3. Data Protection — GDPR Article 28 + International Transfers
For personal data of EU/EEA residents:
- **Controller-processor structure.** Where NEXPEC processes personal data on behalf of an Organization or Client under their Job-related instructions, NEXPEC acts as a **processor** within the meaning of **Article 28 GDPR**. The Data Processing Addendum (DPA-001) constitutes the Article 28 processing agreement and is incorporated by reference into the Tier-2 Role Agreement of every EU-resident controller.
- **International transfer mechanism.** Transfers of personal data from the EU/EEA to NEXPEC (Canada) and to subprocessors are made under the **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) Module Two — Controller to Processor** (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), incorporated by reference and supplemented by the **Transfer Impact Assessment** published at nexpec.com/legal/transfer-impact-assessment.
- **Article 27 EU Representative.** NEXPEC has appointed an Article 27 EU Representative for the purposes of GDPR compliance: **[TBD — appoint before EU market activation]**.
- **Data subject rights.** EU/EEA users may exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making, via privacy@nexpec.com.

## 4. Platform-to-Business Regulation (Reg. 2019/1150)
For Inspector and Agency users established in the EU/EEA, NEXPEC complies with **Regulation (EU) 2019/1150** ("P2B Regulation"):
- **Ranking parameters.** The main parameters used by NEXPEC's matching algorithm and their relative weight are published at nexpec.com/legal/ranking.
- **Restriction, suspension, termination.** NEXPEC will provide a statement of reasons before any restriction, suspension, or termination of an EU-established business user's account, with the timelines required by Articles 4 and 11 of the P2B Regulation.
- **Internal complaint-handling.** EU business users may submit complaints via complaints@nexpec.com.

## 5. Language
The master stack and this Addendum are published in **English**. Member-State official-language versions are provided where mandatory local consumer-protection law so requires (e.g., French for France, German for Germany, Spanish for Spain). The user's accepted-language version controls in any conflict.

## 6. Tax — VAT
Stripe administers VAT registration, invoicing, and remittance via Stripe Tax (including OSS/IOSS where applicable). For the **10% PFT Fee** charged to EU-established business users, the reverse-charge mechanism applies where Stripe Tax determines it applicable. Inspectors operating in the EU are independently responsible for their own VAT registration and reporting per INSP-AGR-001 §2.

## 7. Dispute Resolution — Consumer-Jurisdiction Carve-out
TOS-001 §10 governs disputes (mandatory 30-day mediation → exclusive Montréal courts), **except** that an EU/EEA-resident consumer may bring proceedings against NEXPEC **in the courts of the Member State of the consumer's domicile** (Brussels I bis Regulation Article 18), and NEXPEC may bring proceedings against an EU/EEA-resident consumer **only in the courts of the consumer's Member State of domicile**. The mandatory 30-day mediation step under TOS-001 §10 still applies as a procedural prerequisite where local mandatory law permits.

## 8. Industry-Specific
EU industrial-inspection regimes (e.g., **Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU**, **ATEX 2014/34/EU**) impose certification requirements on Inspectors directly; INSP-AGR-001 §2 applies without modification.
