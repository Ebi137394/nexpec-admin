## Full description (4000 chars)
NEXPEC connects industrial clients with vetted inspection professionals and manages the entire engagement — from first posting to the final, review-ready report.

FOR CLIENTS, AGENCIES AND ENTERPRISES
• Post inspection jobs with scope, site and schedule
• Review applications from verified inspectors — identity disclosure is staged and controlled
• Engage through a contract workflow with in-app signatures
• Receive structured inspection reports with photo evidence
• Track applications, contracts, reports, invoices and communication in one place

FOR INSPECTORS
• Discover work matching your certifications and specialty
• Apply with your professional profile and credentials
• Capture photos, findings, ITP points and voice notes on site
• Submit reports through a senior-review quality process
• Track earnings and request payment release

BUILT FOR ACCOUNTABILITY
• Every professional account is verified by the NEXPEC team before it can trade
• Contracts, reports and key actions carry an audit trail
• Communication runs in controlled channels with in-app reporting
• Commercial terms stay private to the parties involved

AI-ASSISTED TOOLS (BETA)
Optional on-device AI assistance flags potential visual defects for the inspector's attention. AI results are advisory only — every finding is reviewed and confirmed by a qualified human inspector before it enters a report.

PAYMENTS
Engagements are settled by manual payment (bank transfer / invoice), recorded and confirmed in-app by our operations team. Online card payment is coming soon and is shown as unavailable.

Professional accounts (inspector, agency, supplier) require verification by our team. Client accounts can get started right away.
