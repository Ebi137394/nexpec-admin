## Notes
1. Clients can use the app immediately after signup. Inspector/agency/supplier
   accounts enter "verification in progress" until our team approves them —
   deliberate (vetted marketplace).
2. NEXPEC brokers real-world professional inspection services. Under Play's
   payments policy for physical-world services, engagements are settled by
   manual payment (bank transfer / invoice) recorded by our operations team.
   Online card payment is not offered in this release; the payment-options panel notes it as "Coming soon" (informational, not tappable), and Finance is a manual-settlement dashboard. No
   digital goods are sold; Google Play Billing is not required.
3. AI tools (beta) run on-device (TensorFlow Lite) and are advisory only —
   human inspector confirmation is required before anything enters a report.
4. UGC: chat exists only between verified, contracted parties and NEXPEC
   staff; every conversation has an in-app "Report this conversation" control
   routing to our staffed moderation inbox with an audit trail.
5. Google sign-in and Sign in with Apple are offered alongside email/password.
   Password reset deep link: nexpec://reset-password.
6. Account deletion: in-app (Profile → Security → Delete Account) and public
   URL https://www.nexpecapp.com/account/delete
7. Permissions (camera, photos, location, calendar, notifications) are
   requested contextually and the app functions without them.
