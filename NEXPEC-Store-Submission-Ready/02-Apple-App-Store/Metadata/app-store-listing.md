## Description (4000 chars max)
NEXPEC connects industrial clients with vetted inspection professionals and manages the entire engagement — from first posting to the final, review-ready report.

FOR CLIENTS, AGENCIES AND ENTERPRISES
• Post inspection jobs with scope, site and schedule
• Review applications from verified inspectors — identity disclosure is staged and controlled, so you see credentials before contact details
• Engage through a contract workflow with signatures recorded in-app
• Receive structured inspection reports with photo evidence
• Track everything in one place: applications, contracts, reports, invoices and communication

FOR INSPECTORS
• Discover work that matches your certifications and specialty
• Apply with your professional profile, résumé and credentials
• Complete site visits with in-app capture: photos, findings, ITP points and voice notes
• Submit reports through a senior-review quality process
• Track earnings and request payment release

BUILT FOR ACCOUNTABILITY
• Every account is verified by the NEXPEC team before it can trade
• Contracts, reports and key actions carry an audit trail
• Communication runs in controlled, auditable channels with in-app reporting
• Commercial terms stay private to the parties involved

AI-ASSISTED TOOLS (BETA)
NEXPEC includes optional, on-device AI assistance for inspection photography — flagging potential visual defects for the inspector's attention. AI results are advisory only: every finding is reviewed and confirmed by a qualified human inspector before it enters a report.

PAYMENTS
Engagements on NEXPEC are settled by manual payment (bank transfer / invoice), recorded and confirmed in the app by our operations team. Online card payment is coming soon and is shown in-app as unavailable.

NEXPEC is designed for professional use in energy, construction, manufacturing and infrastructure. Creating an inspector, agency or supplier account requires verification by our team; client accounts can get started right away.
