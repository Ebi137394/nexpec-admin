The Problem

Critical inspections run on
email, spreadsheets, and trust.

🕰️

Slow, manual sourcing

Finding a qualified, certified inspector for a specific code and site means days of calls and referrals — with no visibility into availability or price.

📄

Unverifiable results

Findings arrive as PDFs and photos. There's no tamper‑evident chain, no standard, and no way to prove an inspection actually happened as claimed.

📵

Zero connectivity

The work happens on rigs and in refineries with no signal — exactly where today's cloud tools stop working entirely.

The result: wasted time, disputed reports, poached relationships, and safety‑critical data trapped on someone's phone.

The Solution

A two‑sided marketplace
with an AI co‑inspector
built in.

  • ✳Match & book — clients post work; certified inspectors and agencies are matched, quote, and get hired.
  • ✳Capture with AI — the field app runs custom segmentation models fully on‑device to detect welds, corrosion, and coating defects.
  • ✳Seal & prove — every AI finding is cryptographically bound to a signed model version; reports are tamper‑evident.
  • ✳Sync when ready — offline‑first by design; nothing is lost in a dead zone.

Security & Trust

Trust is a feature,
enforced in code.

  • 🔒Row‑level security everywhere — 147 governed tables; a CI guard proves admin coverage on all of them.
  • 🕵️Anti‑poaching by construction — inspector identity & payout economics are structurally hidden from client surfaces; a guard scans every buyer screen for leakage.
