# Canada — Country Addendum

> **Plain-English summary.** This is the Canada overlay on the NEXPEC legal stack. Because NEXPEC is based in Montréal, Québec, most of the master stack already aligns with Canadian law. This Addendum adds the things that are specifically required for Canadian users and Canadian-performed Jobs — Bill 96 French availability, Law 25 and PIPEDA privacy rights, Québec consumer-protection forum carve-out for B2C, and CASL marketing-comms compliance.

## 1. Trigger
This Addendum applies whenever any of the following is true: the user is resident in Canada or registered as a business in Canada; or the Job is performed in Canada.

## 2. Consumer Protection (B2C overlay — Québec)
For Job Contracts where the Client is an individual consumer (not a business) resident in Québec, the **Consumer Protection Act (Québec)** ("CPA") applies notwithstanding TOS-001 §10. The consumer retains all non-waivable CPA rights — including the right to file complaints with the **Office de la protection du consommateur** — and may bring an action before the **Court of Québec, Division of Small Claims** for amounts within its jurisdiction without first completing the mandatory mediation step in TOS-001 §10.

## 3. Data Protection — Law 25 + PIPEDA
For personal information processed in connection with Québec users, **Loi 25 (An Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information)** applies in addition to the Privacy Policy (PRIV-001). For all other Canadian users, **PIPEDA** applies. Specifically:
- **Privacy Officer.** NEXPEC's Privacy Officer is reachable at privacy@nexpec.com and is the contact for both PIPEDA and Law 25 inquiries.
- **Cross-border transfer.** Under Law 25 §17, NEXPEC has assessed the data-protection regime of Stripe (USA) and Supabase / AWS (multi-region) and concluded that contractual safeguards (Stripe's SCC-equivalent commitments and AWS's data-protection terms) provide protection equivalent to that required under Law 25.
- **Data-portability.** Québec users may request data portability beginning on Law 25's portability effective date.
- **Right to de-indexing.** Québec users may request de-indexing of inaccurate, incomplete, or equivocal personal information.

## 4. Language — Bill 96
NEXPEC complies with the **Charter of the French Language** (R.S.Q. c. C-11), as amended by the Act respecting French, the official and common language of Québec ("Bill 96"). Specifically:
- Québec-resident users are offered **French-language versions** of all consumer-facing legal documents (TOS-001, PRIV-001, AUP-001, and the applicable Tier-2 Role Agreement).
- Where the user accepts the **French version**, the French version controls in any conflict between language editions.
- French translations are published as they are completed; until publication, the user's continued use of the Platform constitutes an exception under Charter §1.1 (B2B carve-out) where applicable.

## 5. Marketing & Anti-Spam — CASL
NEXPEC complies with **Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation** (CASL). Commercial electronic messages from NEXPEC require the user's express or implied consent, identify the sender, and provide an unsubscribe mechanism. The user may withdraw CASL consent at any time at privacy@nexpec.com.

## 6. Tax Treatment
Stripe administers GST/HST and QST registration, invoicing, and remittance via Stripe Tax where Canadian tax thresholds are met. Inspectors operating in Canada are independently responsible for their own CRA business-number registration, GST/HST/QST collection (if applicable), and income-tax reporting per INSP-AGR-001 §2.

## 7. Dispute Resolution — Carve-out
TOS-001 §10 governs disputes (mandatory 30-day mediation → exclusive Montréal courts). For Québec B2C disputes, §2 of this Addendum operates as a mandatory carve-out. For all other Canadian disputes (B2B, non-Québec consumer), TOS-001 §10 applies in full.

## 8. Industry-Specific
For Jobs performed in Canada, Inspectors are responsible for their own provincial certifications (e.g., **CSA**, **TSSA**, **Petroleum Technician Certification Board**) and for compliance with **OHS** law in the controlling province (e.g., Loi sur la santé et la sécurité du travail in Québec, OHSA in Ontario).
