# United Kingdom — Country Addendum

> **Plain-English summary.** This is the UK overlay on the NEXPEC legal stack. Post-Brexit the UK diverged from EU GDPR in important ways — most notably, transfers out of the UK use the **IDTA** (International Data Transfer Agreement) rather than the EU SCCs. UK consumer-protection rules and the UK "worker" classification risk also require explicit overlay. NEXPEC must appoint a UK GDPR Article 27 Representative before activating the UK market.

## 1. Trigger
This Addendum applies whenever any of the following is true: the user is resident in the United Kingdom; the user is a business registered in the United Kingdom; or the Job is performed in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Consumer Protection — Consumer Rights Act 2015
For Job Contracts where the Client is an individual consumer (not a business) resident in the UK, the **Consumer Rights Act 2015** ("**CRA 2015**") applies. Specifically:
- **Services with reasonable care and skill (CRA 2015 §49).** The Inspector — not NEXPEC — provides the service. The Inspector warrants under INSP-AGR-001 §3 that performance is rendered with the skill and care ordinarily exercised by qualified inspectors. **NEXPEC does not assume CRA 2015 §49 service obligations** because NEXPEC is not the service provider.
- **Pre-contract information.** Consumer-facing Job Contracts include the pre-contractual information required by Schedule 1 of the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.
- **Statutory rights non-excludable.** Nothing in TOS-001, CLI-AGR-001, or this Addendum excludes or limits any non-excludable statutory right of a UK consumer (CRA 2015 §31, §47, §65).

## 3. Worker-Classification Risk (UK-Specific)
The United Kingdom recognizes an intermediate **"worker"** category between "employee" and "self-employed contractor" (Employment Rights Act 1996 §230(3)(b)). Inspectors operating on the Platform are engaged on terms requiring **independent-contractor** status under INSP-AGR-001 §1. NEXPEC and the Inspector each represent that they do not intend, and do not consider, the relationship to give rise to "worker" status under UK law. **In the event that a court or tribunal determines that worker status applies notwithstanding this Agreement, the indemnity in INSP-AGR-001 §7 expressly extends to that determination and to any consequential entitlement (minimum wage, holiday pay, working-time, statutory rests).** No part of the **10% PFT Fee** constitutes wages, salary, or other payment for labour by NEXPEC.

## 4. Data Protection — UK GDPR + IDTA
For personal data of UK residents:
- **UK GDPR.** UK GDPR (as defined by the Data Protection Act 2018 §3) applies in parallel with the EU GDPR. Article 28 controller-processor obligations mirror the EU framework and are governed by the Data Processing Addendum (DPA-001).
- **International transfer mechanism.** Transfers of personal data from the UK to NEXPEC (Canada) and to subprocessors are made under the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement** (the "**IDTA**", February 2022) and/or the **UK Addendum to the EU SCCs** where transfers cascade through an EU establishment, supplemented by the **Transfer Risk Assessment** published at nexpec.com/legal/transfer-impact-assessment.
- **Article 27 UK Representative.** NEXPEC has appointed a UK GDPR Article 27 Representative: **[TBD — appoint before UK market activation]**.
- **ICO contact.** UK users may lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office** (ICO).

## 5. Language
English. No additional language obligations.

## 6. Tax — VAT (UK)
Stripe administers UK VAT registration, invoicing, and remittance via Stripe Tax. For the **10% PFT Fee** charged to UK-established business users, the reverse-charge mechanism applies where Stripe Tax determines it applicable. Inspectors operating in the UK are independently responsible for their own VAT registration and reporting per INSP-AGR-001 §2.

## 7. Dispute Resolution — Consumer-Jurisdiction Carve-out
TOS-001 §10 governs disputes (mandatory 30-day mediation → exclusive Montréal courts), **except** that a UK-resident consumer retains the right to bring proceedings against NEXPEC **in the courts of the part of the United Kingdom in which the consumer is domiciled**, per the UK's retained consumer-jurisdiction rules under the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (as amended). The mandatory 30-day mediation step still applies as a procedural prerequisite where local mandatory law permits.

## 8. Industry-Specific — HSE & Sector Regulators
For Jobs performed in the UK, Inspectors are responsible for their own certifications under the **Health and Safety Executive** regime, the **Offshore Installations** safety regime where applicable, and sector-specific bodies (e.g., **ONR** for nuclear, **HSE COMAH** for major-hazard sites). NEXPEC does not verify HSE / ONR / COMAH-specific entitlement.
