## Product feature inventory (by audience)

**Inspectors**
- On-device AI co-inspector (defect/corrosion assist) that runs locally — no cloud dependency, evidence stays on device until sync.
- Offline-first field capture with a sync outbox (works with no signal; drains on reconnect).
- Job discovery, bidding, and admin-brokered contracts.
- Protected earnings via a payout-hold ledger; payouts individually reviewed (no black-box automation).
- Price-blindness: you see your payout, never the client's price or platform margin.
- Cryptographically signed evidence packs; pseudonymous public trust card (anti-poaching by construction).

**Clients**
- Post a scope; NEXPEC brokers a vetted specialist (identity protected until engagement).
- Expert/admin-reviewed reports before they reach you.
- Structural price-blindness (you see your price only).
- Spend, invoices, budget overview, and dispute filing in one place.
- Protected payments via Stripe; funds held on payout hold until report sign-off.

**Suppliers**
- Brokered procurement: RFQs, confidential quotes, administered offers (your raw price is never exposed to the buyer).
- Two-party e-signed supplier contracts (contract-before-money).
- Payout-hold earnings ledger and wallet.

**Agencies**
- Multi-seat roster management; team scoped to the organization.
- Agency-level job and report visibility.

**Enterprise teams**
- Organization accounts with team roles, department scoping, and a controller-grade Data Processing Addendum (DPA).
- Audit-grade records and rollup reporting.

**Industrial companies / asset owners**
- A verifiable inspection trail: signed evidence, admin-reviewed reports, immutable audit history.

**QA/QC managers & inspection coordinators**
- Non-conformance reports (NCRs) and flash reports.
- Report revisions and a sealed commercial revision ledger.
- Full audit events on privileged actions.

**Cross-cutting**
- Web + mobile (iOS/Android), 7-language UI.
- Full account controls incl. in-app + web account deletion with business-record retention.
- Owner/admin protections and price-blindness enforced in the database itself.
